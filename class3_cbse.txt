Noun

A noun is the name of a person, place, animal, or thing.

Examples:
Teacher, School, Dog, Book

###
Verb

A verb is an action word.

Examples:
Run, Jump, Play
###
Adjective
An adjective describes a noun.
Examples: Beautiful, Tall, Red
###
